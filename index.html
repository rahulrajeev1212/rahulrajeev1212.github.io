Projects

Synthesis

Solvent-free combustion of Ga-LLZO

Developed a route that forms cubic Ga-doped LLZO powder in 15 minutes at ~90% yield. Scaled batches from 5 g to 1 kg and optimized sintering to 94.2% density and 5.8 × 10⁻⁴ S cm⁻¹ at 30 °C.

Fabrication

3D-printed LLZO ceramic tubes

Formulated a 50 wt.% solids ceramic slurry and tuned print geometry, debinding and reactive-sintering schedules up to 1150 °C. Closed-end tubes reached ~92% density. Delivered 25 tubes to SRNL with no cracking or leakage in flow-meter and gas chromatography leak checks. Invention disclosure filed with the Clemson University Research Foundation.

Degradation

Ga- and Ta-doped LLZO in molten Li–Pb

Combined XRD/Rietveld, SEM/EDS, XPS and TEM to compare the two dopants after exposure. Ga-LLZO transformed from cubic to tetragonal and showed Pb penetration along grain boundaries. Ta-LLZO kept its cubic structure with a ~2 µm reaction layer.

Hardware

Custom high-temperature test systems

Built a multi-sample impedance system (room temperature to 500 °C) and a sealed inert-atmosphere cell for molten Li–Pb exposures at 350–450 °C, up to 100 h. Tested Li|LLZO|Li symmetric cells by EIS, cyclic voltammetry, critical current density and 100 h Li stripping/plating.

Reliability

Glass seals for SOFCs

Screened three sealants for 350–650 °C service with thermal cycling, helium leak testing, DSC, CTE mismatch and adhesion tests. Schott GM31107 was selected.

Figures

Solvent-free combustion synthesis

  • Mix

    Metal nitrates and urea ground together in a mortar until a gel forms. Li at 10% excess.

  • Combust

    Gel in an alumina crucible goes into a preheated furnace for 15 minutes.

  • Collect

    White, fluffy cubic Ga-LLZO powder at ~90% yield. Fuel-to-oxidizer ratio 0.5.

LLZO tube fabrication

  1. MillLi₂CO₃ (20% excess), La₂O₃, ZrO₂ and Ga₂O₃ ball-milled together.
  2. Slurry50 wt.% solids in water with HPMC binder and a dispersant.
  3. PrintLayer-by-layer extrusion into a closed-end tube. A CO₂ laser at 7.5 W dries each layer before the next.
  4. SinterReactive sintering at 1150 °C for 10 h under sacrificial mother powder. Phase formation and densification happen in one step.
